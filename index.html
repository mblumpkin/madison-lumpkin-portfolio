A Little About Me...

Hi! I'm Madison, a student at Louisiana State University in Baton Rouge, pursuing a BA in Arts & Design and an MS in Digital Media Arts & Engineering.

With a foundation in traditional painting and drawing, my practice has evolved into a deep interest in 3D art and digital creation.

I'm especially drawn to animation, character development, modeling, rendering, programming, and texturing. I love combining artistic and technical skills to create engaging experiences and tell stories.

I'm always excited to explore new software, creative processes, and artistic mediums. Outside of my work, I'm a tennis player and coach, a cook, a jewelry maker, a hockey fan, and an avid reader.
